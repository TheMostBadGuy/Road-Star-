Three distinct forces

Grip. Pressure. Impulse.

Suction loads the tires toward a nearby surface. Wing forces depend on airflow. Cold gas supplies thrust and consumes stored mass. Keeping these mechanisms separate makes the simulator interpretable.

01 / Fan suction

Force comes from a pressure difference.

Nearby surfaceSkirts limit leakage
→
Pressure dropFans remove underfloor air
→
Contact loadF ≈ ΔP × sealed area

Gravity still acts downward in the world. Inversion needs enough adhesion to carry vehicle weight and maneuvering load. Lose the seal or electrical power, and support must fall away.

Fan pressure, sealed area, leakage and mode response in this project are calibrated approximations.

02 / Cold-gas thrust

Stored gas is a finite resource.

VesselsPressure + thermal state
→
Valve + nozzleMass flow + expansion
→
Thrust + torqueGas mass decreases

Peak thrust and total firing duration cannot be multiplied casually. Track the force integral, tank state and flow allocation to rear, lift and yaw banks.
