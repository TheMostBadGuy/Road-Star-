# Tesla Roadster (2nd generation, 2026 reveal): research dossier for the 3D model and driving simulator

Compiled 2026-10-24. Every fact has a source and a confidence tag.

**Confidence / provenance tags**
- **[OFFICIAL]**: text or images on tesla.com/roadster, or Tesla's own X account. Read directly on 2026-10-24.
- **[EXEC]**: stated by Tesla executives (Lars Moravy, VP Vehicle Engineering; Franz von Holzhausen, design chief) in the Top Gear, Hagerty or MotorTrend interviews of 2026-10-16. Reached here only through press re-reporting.
- **[MUSK]**: Elon Musk, on stage or on X.
- **[PRESS]**: a press figure, estimate or observation with no clear Tesla attribution.
- **[DERIVED]**: my own measurement or calculation from official images or the physics. The method is shown.
- **[UNKNOWN]**: nobody has published it.

> **Key point:** The car was unveiled on **15 Oct 2026**. Tesla's site carries **no spec sheet**: no dimensions, no kWh, no hp, no top speed, no price. It gives only qualitative copy, one number ("0-60 in under 1 second" for Thrust) and photos. Every other figure comes from executive interviews, and those sources contradict each other (see the conflicts table in section 2.4).

## 4. Exterior design (for the 3D artist)

All points below come from the official tesla.com photos (see reference_images.md) unless a source is cited.

**Overall:** A low, wide mid/rear-heavy wedge with "Cyber"-influenced faceting. The surfaces are curved, but the creases are sharp. The nose is a sharp horizontal blade. The roofline peaks behind the B-point, then runs in a long, nearly straight line to a cut-off tail (Kamm-like vertical rear face). The front of the canopy is a big wraparound windscreen that runs far up into the roof, like a fighter canopy. **No exterior side mirrors are visible in any official shot**, so assume camera-based mirrors. The rear deck has a recessed dark rectangular rear window/engine-cover glass, flanked by two flat "sail" panels (flying-buttress-like) that run to the tail.

**Paint:** **"Ano White"**, aerospace-inspired (UV/cryogenic/electrostatic-dissipative coating). It is a satin/semi-matte warm off-white, not glossy. Use a slightly rough clearcoat, roughness about 0.35. The 2017 prototype was red; the 2026 car has been shown **only in white**. [OFFICIAL]

**Glass:** **"Gold Glazed"**, a copper/gold-tinted glass that mimics spacesuit visors. From outside it reads as warm bronze/brown. **Titanium accents** sit on trim edges. [OFFICIAL]

**Front:**
- A full-width thin white LED light bar runs along the leading edge of the nose, under the hood's front lip.
- Below it is a dark smoked/black fascia band with a front bumper camera (NaTA). A black lower splitter lip extends forward.
- The hood is a flat trapezoid with a separate rectangular center panel, which is the **hood vent** (it opens).
- The front fenders rise as sharp peaked "wings" on each side of the hood. **Hidden pop-down headlights** sit in triangular pockets at the inboard top corner of each fender peak, where fender meets hood. A panel drops down to reveal slim LED high/low beams. Lit, they show a small horizontal multi-LED array. [OFFICIAL; Motor1 2026-10-21]
- **No frunk.** The nose opens as a front wing. [EXEC]

**Side:**
- The front wheel arch is tight and round, with a sharp crease above.
- Immediately behind the front wheel, at the door's leading edge, are an **amber side-marker strip** and a **black vertical slot**, which is where the **barge board** deploys (UI callout).
- The door's lower edge rises toward the rear, and the dark lower body/sill (black) gets taller toward the back.
- The DLO (side glass) is a long wedge whose lower edge rises toward the rear. There is a thin vertical seam about mid-door (the door/rear-quarter cut line).
- The rear haunch is broad, with a flat faceted panel over the rear wheel.
- **Doors:** butterfly/scissor (dihedral) doors that open up and forward, hinged near the A-pillar. Each door includes part of the roof glass. They are **manually operated** (NaTA: "manual scissor-style"; Wikipedia: butterfly). [EXEC/PRESS]

**Rear:**
- A **full-width thin red light bar** spans the tail. A second red light strip on the wing makes a doubled red line when the wing is up.
- The license plate area is centered, with an embossed silver plate reading **"RDSTR"**.
- Below the plate: **4 round thruster nozzles** in a row with gold/brass-lined bells (Thrust and Rocket), above a **carbon-fiber box housing 4 axial suction fans** divided by 3 strakes.
- The lower diffuser has horizontal slats. Rocket adds downward nozzles at the rear corners, just inboard of the rear wheels.
- **Rear wing:** a wide, flat chord on **two swan-neck struts** mounted near the center, with a bronze/titanium-tint finish and short end plates. It deploys up and rearward from the tail deck, and stows flush as the trailing panel of the rear deck.

**Wheels:** **6-spoke**, thin, gloss-black faceted blade spokes. Each spoke has a bright machined/titanium chamfer on one edge. The spokes sit over **flat light-gray/white aero disc inserts** that fill the gaps between them, creating 6 triangular aero panels. A round center cap carries a Roadster/Tesla emblem. Gold brake hardware is visible behind. Tires are Pirelli P Zero Trofeo with white "TROFEO" sidewall script. The front wheels look slightly smaller (20 in vs 21 in).

**Badging:** No large exterior "TESLA" script is visible. There is the **"ROADSTER" wordmark** (Tesla page logo asset) and the **"RDSTR" plate**. The wheel caps carry an emblem.
